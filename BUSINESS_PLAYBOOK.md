## 1. 🕒 How It Actually Works in Real Life (A Typical Tuesday on Phadke Road)

Many doctors hesitate to adopt tech because they think *"My receptionist is not tech-savvy"* or *"Elderly patients won't download an app"*. SlotCraft requires **zero app downloads** and runs on existing phones and laptops.

### The Real-Life Flow:
1. **6:15 PM:** Commuter boards Dombivli fast local at Thane or CSMT. Opens link on phone & books 7:30 PM slot. Receives live Token #06 on phone.
2. **6:45 PM:** Clinic evening rush begins on Phadke Road. Receptionist calls Token #03 into chamber. Doctor completes checkup, adds notes & Rx, clicks "Complete & Call Next".
3. **7:10 PM:** Train reaches Dombivli Platform 1. Patient's phone shows "2 Patients Ahead (~20m wait)". Patient grabs tea or fresh vegetables on Phadke Road instead of sitting in a cramped clinic waiting room.
4. **7:25 PM:** Receptionist clicks one-tap WhatsApp: "Token #06 called, please enter!". Patient walks 2 mins into clinic right on time.

### The 3 Patient Personas in Real Life:
1. **The Commuter (Smart Booking):** Books from mobile browser, tracks live queue while on Central Railway local, arrives exactly when 1 patient remains ahead.
2. **The Walk-In (Elderly / Traditional):** Walks in without a phone. Receptionist types their name in 5 seconds (`Add Walk-In`). Their number appears on the Waiting Room TV board.
3. **The Delayed Commuter:** Train is running late; patient taps *"Running Late? Add +15m Buffer"*. The system swaps them with the next waiting patient automatically—no angry calls to reception.
